Good morning. For the first time in nearly 30 years, Texas, Alabama and Georgia all lost on the same Saturday. Amid a wild weekend in college football, your Fightin’ Texas Aggies took care of their business. It’s Monday, October 19 — here’s what you need to know to start your day.

The Big 3
1
 Football blanks The Citadel, 41-0: Texas A&M posted its second shutout of the season Saturday, the first time since 1993 the Aggies have had two in one year. Jamarion Morrow ran for three touchdowns, freshman Aaron Gregory caught two, and A&M piled up 501 yards to move to 4-3. Next up: Saturday at No. 13 Alabama, 2:30 p.m. on ABC. Link
2
 Volleyball rebounds on Senior Day: No. 19 volleyball snapped a three-match losing streak yesterday with a 3-1 win over Auburn at Reed Arena. Kyndal Stowers had 21 kills, four Aggies reached double digits, and freshman Gabi Rodriguez logged her first career double-double. A&M is 11-8, 4-3 in the SEC, with Georgia on Friday and No. 4 Kentucky on Sunday. Link
3
 Both golf teams tee off this morning: Men’s golf plays the inaugural Abilene Christian Intercollegiate at Halbert National, with Golf Channel live from 3 p.m. to 6 p.m. each day. No. 7 women’s golf, the highest-ranked team in the field, opens The Fin with a 9:30 a.m. shotgun start. Both events run 54 holes through Wednesday. Link, Link

The Rundown

Soccer

Aggies Fall to LSU on Late Penalty, 4-3

Ireland Churchill scored twice and Kieana Smith added her first collegiate goal, but LSU tied it in the 80th minute and won on a penalty with 50 seconds left. A&M (4-10-1) is at Missouri Thursday at 8 p.m. on SEC Network. Link

Volleyball

Volleyball Drops a Four-Setter to No. 7 Florida

Friday night went 3-1 Florida, with the Aggies taking the second set 25-23. Kyndal Stowers had 17 kills and 11 digs, Eliza Sharp returned from injury with eight kills and four blocks, and 6,464 filled Reed Arena, fifth-most in program history. Link

Cross Country

Moiben Finishes Second at Arturo Barrios Invitational

Michael Moiben was second in the men’s 8k in his season debut Friday, and Jack Johnston took seventh. The men placed fifth of 38 teams, ahead of regionally ranked No. 4 TCU and No. 5 Texas. The women were seventh. SEC Championships are Oct. 30. Link

Baseball

Fall Ball Closes With a 13-10 Win Over McNeese

Baseball wrapped fall play Friday night at Blue Bell Park, a 12-inning 13-10 win. Eleven pitchers combined for 22 strikeouts, and Cole Travers hit the lone home run in the 10th. Opening Day is Feb. 19 against Milwaukee. Link
