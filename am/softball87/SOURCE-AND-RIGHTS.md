## Championship and deep-cut narrative

The contemporary NCAA News, May 27, 1987, reports that Carrie Heightley's sixth-inning double to left-center scored Judy Trussell for the sole run in Andaya's perfect game against UCLA. Freshman Julie Smith went 3-for-4 in the deciding game and equaled the then Division I series record with nine hits. Andaya followed her perfect game by allowing two hits and one run in the championship clincher. This supports an ensemble story: the senior pitcher, senior captain, the decisive hitter, and the freshman second baseman.

Source: https://ncaanewsarchive.s3.amazonaws.com/1987/19870527.pdf

NCAA historical championship table independently confirms 56–8, Bob Brock, 4–1 over UCLA, Omaha: https://www.ncaa.com/history/softball/d1

Texas A&M's 2006 NCAA anniversary recognition states Andaya led the country in wins (36) and strikeouts (326), earned three All-America honors, and was named to the NCAA's 25th anniversary team: https://12thman.com/news/2006/4/19/205221535

Judy Trussell Whillock's official Hall of Fame bio calls her a senior captain and co-team MVP alongside Andaya; she had played on the 1984 and 1986 runners-up. Do not repeat the bio's odd graduate-assistant chronology without checking: https://12thman.com/honors/texas-am-athletic-hall-of-fame/judy-whillock/253

Erika Eriksson's official Hall of Fame bio documents her later service to the Lettermen's Association and founding the Aggie Legends Golf Classic with Robert Wilson. ### Discrepancies and editorial decisions

- The current 64-game schedule and original final report reconcile to 56 wins and eight losses. The 2025 record book includes an extra March 13 Central Michigan entry; do not combine the lists or call it 65 games.
- The NCAA digitized final box shows A&M as 55–9. Official season report and championship history establish 56–8. The prototype uses 56–8.
- NCAA digital WCWS summary assigns Andaya one earned run and 0.15 ERA, but individual game boxes charge earned runs in multiple games. Tournament ERA is withheld until original scoring records are reconciled. Season ERA is independently verified in the original final report.
- The NCAA digital final box shows 10 Aggie hits, confirmed by the contemporary NCAA News box score. A separate contemporary newspaper account describes nine. The prototype uses 10 and avoids borrowing uncorroborated newspaper details.
- Live migrated schedule puts same-day WCWS games in an incorrect order. Prototype orders UCLA loss before Nebraska win on May 23, and perfect game before title clincher on May 24, from NCAA tournament chronology.
- The action image of Andaya has no confirmed exact capture date. It is not represented as a 1987 championship image.
- Then-and-now portraits and interviews have not been created or obtained. The current display is an explicitly marked commission plan, not a claim about anyone's present life or a confirmed reunion.
